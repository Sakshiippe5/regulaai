# Information Security Policy (Fictional Sample Bank)
Version 3.2 | Approved by the Board on 15 January 2025 | Review cycle: every 24 months

## 1. Governance
1.1 The Board of Directors approves the Information Security Policy.
1.2 The Bank has appointed a Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), who reports to the Managing Director.
1.3 The policy is reviewed every 24 months or after a major incident, whichever is earlier.

## 2. Vulnerability management
2.1 A vulnerability assessment of all internet-facing systems is carried out once a year by an external vendor.
2.2 Critical findings are remediated within 30 days.
