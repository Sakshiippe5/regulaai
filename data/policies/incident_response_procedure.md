# Cyber Incident Response Procedure (Fictional Sample Bank)
Version 1.4 | Owner: CISO | Last updated: 3 March 2025

## 1. Detection and escalation
1.1 Any suspected cyber incident must be reported to the CISO within 2 hours of detection.
1.2 The CISO classifies the incident as low, medium, or high severity.

## 2. External reporting
2.1 High severity incidents are reported to the regulator within 24 hours of detection.
2.2 Customer communication is decided case by case by the Managing Director.
